STORAGE SAFETY CHECKLIST

5 Golden Rules Before Locking Your Unit

Follow these simple steps before closing your roll-up door to make future access fast and keep your belongings spotless.

1
Clean and Dry Everything

Ensure garden equipment is wiped clean, fuel tanks drained, and refrigerator defrost lines fully dried.

2
Create an Access Aisle

Leave a 2-foot walking path down the middle of your locker so you never have to unpack everything to reach back boxes.

3
Heavy Items on Bottom, Light on Top

Stack large durable bins on the floor to prevent crushing smaller cardboard cartons underneath.

4
Label on All Four Sides

Write box numbers and room tags on multiple faces so you can identify contents no matter how they are rotated.
